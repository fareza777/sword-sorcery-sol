# Complete walkthrough (spoilers)

## 1 · A missing morning

Inspect the forge and take Mira's sketch and supplies. Her star mark means she left a trail deliberately. Ask the market healer about the soldiers, then give her the balm from the forge to finish **A small kindness**. Inspect the east-gate wheel ruts to recover a royal badge. Promise the gatekeeper to bring back the truth if you wish. Both the sketch and badge are required to leave.

## 2 · The green road

Trust Rook and keep your sword sheathed for an early relationship gain. Free the fox in the willow grove with stamina or mana to complete **A tangled traveler**. At the bridge, face the brambleback. Counter its high sweep with Guard, evade its charge with Step Aside, and strike while it is open. Cut its chain afterward for Rook's trust. Show the badge to the ferryman or help repair the ferry. Both the road and passage must be secured.

## 3 · Copper & kindness

Share the clan's meal. The clan is governed by three keepers, and its copper workers want payment, safe roads and repaired floodgates. Promise to clear their name at the council for the alliance route. At the waterwheel, close the sluice before recovering the copper bell; freezing the current is another solution. Help Pip recover the reed whistle to finish **A tune to come home to**. Listen at the memory tree to finish **Names on the copper**. The whistle later avoids the guardian duel entirely.

## 4 · The honest spell

Ask Iona to help undo her mistake. Help her apprentice change the lantern wick for **An unfinished lesson** and two tonics. Find Varric's signed orders in the study and agree to tell the whole truth about Iona's role. The first rune sequence is **Leaf → Water → Sun**. The free hint and the inventory diagram contain the solution. Both the open rune stair and signed orders are required to leave.

## 5 · Behind the banners

Show Varric the orders so the guards doubt him. Duel him by dodging the thrust, guarding the high cut, then striking his open guard. Let the guards take his weapon. Visit the gardener after the duel and return his son's page to complete **The gardener's son** and receive the workshop key. Use the key to free Mira, or pick her lock/melt the hinge with stamina/mana. Ask her to show you how to stop Varric; listening respects her agency. Mira left the clues and secretly weakened the guardian's chain.

## 6 · What a crown is worth

On the balcony, ask what Mira wants for the valley. At the guardian, play Pip's whistle to break the chain peacefully, or survive the guardian's tactical duel. Restore the second seal with **Water → Leaf → Sun**. Both chain and seal objectives are required for the final confrontation.

## Recovery and encounters

Travel uses one stamina. Rest is always available on a map: +4 stamina and +2 mana. Bread restores four stamina; tonic restores four mana; balm removes one wound. A wound reduces maximum stamina by one, up to three wounds. Losing a duel sends you to safety; rest and retry. No failure spends real money or requires an advertisement.

Each chapter has up to two distinct encounters with deterministic random timing. Helping a cart driver, sharing rainwater, trading with a merchant, mending a glass bird, speaking to a doubtful guard and sharing Mira's oranges have persistent inventory/resource/relationship effects. An autosave preserves the pending encounter and its next destination, so reloading never rerolls it.

## Expansion side quests and camp

1. **What she left behind:** examine all three clues at the forge and all three at the gate. Both inspections are required.
2. **The broken sentinel:** at the forest shrine, repair its warning bell with wire, or win the optional sentinel duel. Kind repair earns more trust.
3. **A working river:** at the river lock open the upper and lower gates, leave the middle gate shut. In the copper archive inspect all three records, preserve the receipt, then join the lantern festival.
4. **Light for someone else:** at the observatory turn mirrors 1, 2 and 4 once from their starting positions. The final four mirror states are /, \, /, \. Repair the glass bird in the aviary as well.
5. **A soldier’s choice:** inspect all three kitchen clues and free Wren from the cell. Let him testify. At the dock preserve the sealed evidence for the river-charter ending.
6. **Three small promises:** after rescuing Mira, visit the bell forge in chapter six. Heat → Shape → Cool. A wrong order costs one stamina and resets the sequence; there is no timer.

The tent button on any map opens camp. Gather once per chapter for two stamina, rest freely, trade two silver for supplies, or craft bread, balm, tonic and sun shards from listed ingredients. Each chapter has its own optional personal conversation. Resolved clues, crafting costs, gathering and conversations persist through autosave.

A companion with at least two trust can help once per duel: Rook creates pressure and restores a step, Iona strengthens the guard and mana, Mira finds a weak link. A sun shard pays for one Sun Ward automatically before mana is used.

Open Memories on the title screen or at camp to revisit unlocked artwork in full, read six world notes and track eight milestones. Discoveries survive a new journey.

## Evidence and consequences in 1.2

During investigations, tap the numbered objects on the illustration. The eye button hides or shows these markers; named controls below the art provide the same interactions. Open **Put the evidence together** from the journal, satchel or pause menu. Select two cards, even across card pages, and connect them. An incorrect pair costs nothing. Established connections persist.

| Pair | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Dry goblin cloth + Mira's chalk star | Proves the accusation was planted. Explain this to Tessa at the market. |
| Escort badge + signed orders | Connects the escort to Varric; his guards begin to doubt him. |
| Copper trade leaf + escort badge | Connects the accepted delivery to the royal escort. Show it at the clan council after restoring the river. Both clues are available before leaving the river chapter. |
| Wren's testimony + signed orders | Gives the guards a witness who can confront their commander. |
| Seal diagram + twin sun pendant | Explains how to loosen the guardian's binding during a safe opening. |

If you helped Tessa's patients and explained the planted cloth, ask for her help at the palace guard post. She brings a driver who saw the escort. With the linked badge/orders, the village witness can persuade Varric to surrender. Wren and his linked testimony provide a separate peaceful route. Neither witness requires winning a duel first.

Restoring the river and showing the linked trade evidence earns the river bell and repair wire. Returning the original trade record from the archive or council earns trust, three silver and cheaper wire, but consumes the receipt required by the river-charter ending. Its established connection remains in your evidence board. At the dock, returning tools immediately restores people's work and earns wire/trust; breaking their seals sacrifices the sealed cargo evidence. Keeping the crates sealed preserves the court route. These are explicit tradeoffs.

Help also changes the places you revisit: Tessa's market reopens and bread costs one silver, a repaired sentinel makes forest rest restore five stamina, and the working river has a new illustration. The pause menu's **The valley remembers** lists your current effects.

## Contextual inventory and equipment

The bag button on a scene illustration opens only useful item actions for that location. It can apply the badge at the ferry, wire at the shrine, key at the workshop or whistle at the guardian using the same story choices and costs. At either rune puzzle, the diagram prepares the first two runes once; choose the final rune yourself.

Completing Tessa's medicine quest gives the sunleaf shoulder guard. The river council's evidence scene gives the river promise bell. Equip either from the satchel while safe. The guard prevents one lost guard point per duel; the bell restores one mana on the first perfect counter. Both are visible on Rowan during battles, and both can be removed without consuming them.

## Tactical openings

The **Tactics** button changes stance before a move. Balanced uses normal costs. Guarded blocks one additional hit when guarding but reduces strike pressure by one. Light-footed dodges for one stamina but strikes cost three. Focused wards deal three pressure for three mana. A sun shard still pays for a ward before mana is spent.

Read the movement to spend one round, regain one stamina and mana and add one pressure to the next action. This bonus cannot stack. The round limit still applies. At half remaining enemy pressure, phase two begins: low feints require a dodge, while the guardian's binding flare calls for a Sun Ward. The visible warning and replayable VO explain each move.

Two correct counters or observations build a calm opening. With the enemy at half pressure or below, Tactics can offer a peaceful finish:

| Opponent | Additional requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Brambleback | Two stamina to loosen its chain. |
| Sentinel | One copper wire to repair its bell. |
| Guardian | Linked diagram/pendant evidence and one mana. |
| Varric | Linked badge/orders and Wren/orders evidence. |

Normal victory, retreat after defeat and the earlier noncombat story choices remain available. The four opponents have distinct arenas. Trusted companions now enter the battle illustration when helping, with a bell, ward or tool effect appropriate to their role.
