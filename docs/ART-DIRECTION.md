# Visual direction

The final user direction is a **bright** fantasy world and a **simple, exciting** story. Forest greens, golden sunlight, turquoise rivers, warm ivory architecture, flowers, craft and lively villages replace the original dark-fantasy premise.

Humans use a mixture of natural rear views, silhouettes, occasional closed helmets, and front/three-quarter views with upper faces completely obscured by low hair, hoods or solid ink shadow. In the latter treatment, **only the mouth is visible**. Human eyes and noses must never be visible. Do not put every person in a mask or turn every person away. Animals and monsters may show their whole faces and eyes, per the user's later clarification.

Distinct character cues: Rowan wears teal and carries a sheathed sword; Mira wears a copper smith apron and mustard scarf; Iona wears ivory, sage and a saffron hood; Rook carries river reeds and copper craftwork; Varric uses bronze royal armor. These cues keep characters identifiable when eyes are hidden.

Each image is shown in full, using `object-fit: contain`. No cinematic zoom or cover crop hides the illustration. The menu's portrait key art and the story's landscape panels have separate assets.

Painterly illustrations were generated independently for scene-specific subjects. `tools/art-sources.json` records original generation filenames; `tools/import_art.py` converts the complete image to WebP without cropping, compositing or removing alpha. All 108 art assets are generated raster artwork. Version 1.2 adds 42 assets and replaces the forge investigation image with an illustration showing its three actual clues. The additions include three arenas, three changed-world scenes, eight full-body character poses, a kitchen clue scene, 21 inventory objects, two equipment pieces and four environmental props. Only the UI emblem and interface icons remain vector. `tools/art-plan-v1.1.json` and `tools/art-plan-v1.2.json` preserve prompts, canonical cast rules and generation provenance. Generation used the built-in ImageGen tool, one independent asset per request. `tools/audio_assets.py` regenerates only the original score.

All text and buttons must remain readable on a phone. Default dialogue is 19–20px, visible labels are at least 15px and the larger-text setting remains available. Story dialogue is paginated into short, justified passages. Choices appear one at a time on compact screens and two at a time on taller screens, with explicit Previous/More Choices controls. Map destinations paginate two at a time. Optional inventory, evidence, settings and reference sheets can scroll.

Battle actors preserve generated alpha transparency and appear above the full, uncropped arena. Breathing, sword lunge, guard, dodge, magical ward and enemy recoil are animated with CSS transforms. All animation respects both the motion setting and OS reduced-motion preference. Scene images never zoom or crop. Environment-only compositions omit the cast reference so missing characters cannot appear before their rescue.

The forest bridge uses moss, stone and timber; the shrine uses old roots and repaired copper; the royal court uses ivory and bronze; the crown chamber uses a warm sun mosaic. Goblin locations emphasize reeds, turquoise waterways, communal tables and copper craft. Changed-world illustrations show a reopened market, restored crossing and working river.

Conversation characters use an intact generated full-body image with subtle clipped duplicate overlays for hair, cloak, hand and mouth motion. This is lightweight 2D presentation rather than skeletal animation. The original full image remains visible underneath; only the duplicates are masked. Mouth animation follows bundled VO playback. Equipment appears over Rowan's established battle model. Hotspots and environmental overlays fit the actual contained image bounds, including letterboxing.
